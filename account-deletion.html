Request deletion in the app

  • Sign in to your Rubati account.
  • Open Profile, then Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Select Delete Account, read the disclosure and confirm only if you want to end the account.

The server checks the signed-in account. Completed account removal cannot be undone through the normal app. Signing out, uninstalling or clearing local data does not delete the account. If the app reports cleanup pending, an error or an unclear outcome, contact support rather than assume completion. Do not create another account just to follow up.

Request deletion without the app

Email rubatiapp.ke@gmail.com with the subject Rubati account deletion request. Ask to delete your account and associated personal data; include the email used for Rubati and send from it where possible. You do not need to reinstall the app or create another account.

Support must verify ownership before acting. If you cannot use the registered email, explain that briefly; proportionate follow-up may be needed. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, National ID images, authentication tokens or payment credentials. A reason for leaving is not required.

This mailbox is actively monitored for privacy and account-deletion requests. This page provides an email request route, not an automatic web deletion form. Sending an email does not itself complete deletion. Ownership must be verified and the request processed. No fixed completion deadline is promised here. Reply through the same support route to ask about an outstanding request.

What the account-deletion process removes

The process disables the account and redacts profile name, email, phone and avatar information. It removes eligible profile/public-listing image files, account notifications, push registrations, Saved Homes, notification preferences and alerts. Owned listings are removed from discovery. Limited internal account/property/unit identifiers and details can remain as restricted financial or audit records; these are not an active public profile.

Disabling the account alone is not completed deletion. The installed recovery process records completion only after required file removal and authentication-account removal have been checked. Interrupted cleanup remains pending and requires recovery. This does not promise that every piece of information in financial evidence, shared conversations or backups disappears immediately.

What can remain and why

  • Messages and eligible attachments you sent: the retention rule requires removal 180 days after verified account-deletion completion, unless an authorized documented legal hold applies. During retention they remain visible to the other authorized participant. Your displayed identity becomes Deleted User; original content and timestamps remain and can contain personal details. The other participant's own messages are not removed under your account's clock. Shared files may remain while another authorized retained message needs them. Independently saved copies and delivered notifications cannot be recalled.
  • Identity images: the retention rule requires removal 90 days after reliable final approval or rejection, except where longer retention is reasonably necessary for a documented, authorized dispute, fraud/security investigation or legal obligation. Genuinely active pending applications do not start this countdown. Reopening does not silently reset it. Abandoned images become eligible after 90 consecutive days of genuine inactivity and only after advance notice, explicit acknowledgment of the specific notice and a further seven full days. Receiving or opening a notice is not acknowledgment. Unacknowledged notices never authorize automatic deletion. Genuine activity resets inactivity; active reviews, unresolved appeals, missing historical evidence and authorized holds remain protected. Account deletion does not invent a final decision or acknowledge an abandonment notice.
  • Reports/decisions: the retention rule requires removal of dismissed details 180 days after reliable final review; resolved details and moderation decision audits after 365 days. Authorized holds and uncertain/reopened cases require review. Report expiry does not reverse active blocks or moderation decisions.
  • Financial/audit evidence: charges, payment references/snapshots, commissions, payouts and necessary referral/account/listing anchors may remain for justified financial, security, dispute or legal needs. Restrict/minimize personal details when unnecessary. Exact periods require final review; indefinite retention is not approved by default.
  • Backups/technical recovery records: active-service removal does not immediately erase every backup. Separate access, lifecycle and restoration controls are needed; no backup duration is invented.

The retention safeguards are installed, but automatic identity-image, message and moderation cleanup and abandonment notices remain disabled. No automatic retention-deletion schedules are active. The periods above describe retention rules, not a claim that scheduled removal is operating. Account-deletion requests are a separate process. Contact support about retained data, a specific exception or unfinished cleanup.
