1. Operator and contact

Rubati is operated by Alex Kinyua Mbiti, the data controller for the app, serving people in Kenya. Support/privacy contact: rubatiapp.ke@gmail.com. This mailbox is actively monitored for privacy and account-deletion requests. Rubati helps tenants discover rentals, landlords and agents advertise properties, and users communicate.

2. Current retention operation

Rubati's account-deletion and retention safeguards have been installed. Automatic identity-image, deleted-user message and moderation-record cleanup are currently disabled, as are automatic abandonment notices. No automatic retention-deletion schedules are active. The periods below describe Rubati's retention rules; they do not mean scheduled removal is currently operating. Contact rubatiapp.ke@gmail.com for a deletion or retention request. Account-deletion requests and automatic timed-retention cleanup are separate processes.

3. Accounts and profiles

Rubati processes name, email, phone, account type, optional profile photo, authentication identifiers and session information to manage accounts, confirm email, recover access and protect the service. Terms/privacy acknowledgement versions and timestamps are recorded. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication; never send a password to support.

Relevant lister name, contact phone, photo, property-specific Landlord or Agent role and account-level verification status may be displayed with eligible listings. Profiles are not an unrestricted public directory.

4. Listings, searches and location

Listers supply property names, descriptions, county/town/area/address, coordinates, photographs, unit details, rent, deposit and availability. Eligible published information is displayed for discovery. Listing photos use public URLs and may remain accessible by URL after unpublishing until removed. Do not upload identity documents or private information as listing photos.

Search criteria are processed to find homes; Saved Homes are associated with your account. Device location is requested when you choose Near Me or current-location alerts and grant permission. Alert location, area, source and radius are saved for nearby alerts. Exact property coordinates have restricted access; approximate location supports discovery. The current app does not continuously track device location in the background.

5. Landlord Verification

Optional verification collects official names, National ID information, front/back identity-card images, declarations, consent and review records for account identity review. Authorized Rubati administrators can review them. Images are held in private Storage and are not public listing content.

Verified Landlord means the account completed Rubati's current identity-verification process. It is not Property Verification or a guarantee of property ownership, listing accuracy, conduct or transaction safety. Paying the verification fee does not itself confer verified status.

6. Messages and attachments

Rubati stores message text, attachments, participants, property/unit context, conversation identifiers, timestamps and read state to deliver conversations and unread indicators. The other authorized participant can read messages sent to them. Messaging is not end-to-end encrypted.

Authorized administrators can inspect reported message evidence within the reporting system's access and retention limits. This is not unrestricted routine monitoring. Retained text, files and supplied context can contain personal information. After account deletion, the displayed conversation identity becomes Deleted User; this does not remove identifiers typed into a message. Copies saved by another participant and already delivered notifications cannot be recalled.

7. Payments and Affiliate information

Rubati records listing charges, M-Pesa transaction references, amounts, administrator-observed payment date/time, verification outcomes, immutable confirmation evidence and history. M-Pesa transfers take place separately. Submitting a code is not payment confirmation or listing publication. The current app does not collect payment-card details.

The optional Affiliate programme processes applications, contact/payout details, programme acceptance, referral codes, attributed landlords, qualifying commissions, payout requests and history. The relevant Affiliate sees attributed landlord/commission information, not arbitrary profiles or private messages. Admins review and record payouts manually. These records support programme operation, disputes and financial evidence.

8. Notifications, reports and blocks

Notification preferences, Firebase push tokens, installation identifiers, platform information and delivery outcomes support requested notifications. Message/nearby alerts depend on preferences and device permission. Push previews may appear on the lock screen depending on Android settings; delivery is not guaranteed.

The reporting/blocking system processes report reasons, optional explanations, referenced content/users, reporter identity, review notes/decisions, blocks and justified holds. Reports and Admin notes are private to authorized reviewers. Reports do not automatically suspend accounts or remove listings. Blocks prevent new direct messages in both directions while preserving authorized conversation history.

9. Purposes and processing grounds

Information is used for requested account, listing, discovery, messaging and programme services; payment/identity review; requested alerts; reports/abuse handling; user support; account security; and evidence where a lawful obligation or justified dispute/security need applies.

Relevant grounds depend on the activity: providing requested services, consent where relied on for optional processing, proportionate legitimate interests such as security/abuse prevention, and applicable legal obligations where they require processing. Device permission is not a substitute for required legal consent. Withdrawal affects consent-based processing, not records retained on a separate lawful basis.

10. Providers, recipients and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, database, file Storage and server infrastructure. Google Firebase, including Firebase Cloud Messaging, supports notifications. Brevo is the configured provider for authentication emails, such as signup confirmation and password recovery.

Providers process information needed for their services. This policy does not claim all processing stays in Kenya or that a particular transfer agreement has been verified.

These pages contain no additional analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts or web deletion form. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for its processing practices. Emailing support uses your email service and Rubati's support mailbox.

11. Security

Rubati uses authenticated access, database access policies, private Storage and HTTPS service connections. Listing images intentionally have public access. Sessions, request information and error/security logs support service operation and protection. Service providers can keep their own operational logs. No internet service is risk-free; these measures are not an absolute security guarantee.

12. Retention and deletion

Rubati's retention rules are:

  • Identity images: the policy requires removal 90 days after the application's reliable final approval or rejection decision, except where longer retention is reasonably necessary for a documented, authorized dispute, fraud/security investigation or legal obligation. Genuinely active pending applications do not start this countdown. Reopening does not silently reset an existing final-decision deadline.
  • Abandoned verification images: become eligible after 90 consecutive days without genuine applicant or authorized review activity. Deletion also requires advance notice, the applicant's explicit acknowledgment of that specific notice and a further seven full days. Opening a notice or receiving a push/email is not acknowledgment. Resuming genuine activity resets the inactivity calculation. Unacknowledged notices never authorize automatic deletion. Active reviews, unresolved appeals and authorized legal holds remain protected; missing historical activity or notice evidence requires review.
  • Deleted-user messages and eligible attachments: the policy requires removal 180 days after durable, verified account-deletion completion unless an authorized documented legal hold applies. During retention, the other authorized participant may read them. The other participant's own messages are not deleted under your account's clock. Shared files are removed only when no other authorized retained message still needs them.
  • Dismissed report details: the policy requires removal 180 days after reliable final review. Resolved report details and moderation decision audits: 365 days after reliable final review. Authorized documented holds can defer removal. Reopened or historically uncertain records require review, not invented dates.

Automatic timed cleanup remains disabled as explained above; this policy does not promise that it is already deleting data. Interrupted removal needs recovery and verification. Identity review decisions and necessary financial evidence are separate from identity images. Report expiry does not reverse active blocks or account/listing moderation.

Financial evidence, necessary referral/account/listing audit records and minimal cleanup proof may remain where reasonably needed for service history, a dispute, security or a legal obligation. These records can include personal information needed for that purpose and are separate from public profile information. Fixed retention periods for these records, backups and provider logs have not been finalized; indefinite retention is not authorized by default. Applicants who cannot be contacted or cannot acknowledge a notice remain protected from automatic abandonment deletion. Backups can contain older data: deleting active records does not immediately erase every backup. Restricted access and separate backup-lifecycle controls are needed, and restoration must not silently return expired information to active use.

13. Account deletion and your rights

Signed-in users can initiate deletion through Profile → Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account and review the confirmation. Alternatively email rubatiapp.ke@gmail.com asking to delete the account and associated data, including the account email. Reinstallation is not required. Ownership must be verified; follow-up may be needed if you cannot use the registered email. Never email passwords, one-time codes, ID images or payment credentials.

The account-deletion process disables the account, redacts profile contact information, removes eligible profile/public-listing image files and account notifications/saves/alerts, and removes owned listings from discovery. Completion is recorded only after required file removal and authentication-account removal have been checked. Necessary financial/audit records remain separate. An interrupted or pending response is not confirmation of completed deletion. Uninstalling or signing out is not account deletion.

Read the Account Deletion page for steps and retained data. No unverified completion deadline is promised; specific legitimate retention obligations may require follow-up.

Subject to Kenyan law, you may seek information about processing, access, correction, objection, deletion, withdrawal of consent where relied on and other applicable remedies. Email a brief request with only necessary information. You can complain to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. This policy does not limit statutory rights.
